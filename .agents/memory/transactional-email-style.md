---
name: Simix email visual style
description: Durable visual rules shared by transactional emails and admin campaigns.
---

Simix campaign emails follow the supplied mobile-email reference: pale gray page, rounded white card, small blue brand link, large black title, readable black Arial/Helvetica text and restrained blue links. Avoid dark banners and oversized CTA buttons.

**Why:** Email clients strip or inconsistently support external CSS and webfonts; inline styles and a system font stack keep the professional reference look reliable in Gmail, Outlook, and mobile clients.

**How to apply:** Keep admin campaign rendering and its panel preview aligned with the reference. Preserve generous spacing and minimal decoration, escape dynamic values, and never expose codes in logs.
